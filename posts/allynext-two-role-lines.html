2025 年的两处角色，我都照原样留着 Both 2025 role wordings stay as written

2025 年 3 月到 12 月是 AllyNext。回看这一年，角色有两行字，我都留着。项目条目写的是 AI 产品负责人。工作经历里，同一段写成深度参与 AIGC Agent 的全链路。条目上的负责人，不拿来擦掉经历里的「深度参与」。经历里的参与，也不拿来否定条目上的负责人。

两行字各管一种说法

写「负责人」，指的是项目记录里的那一行。写「深度参与」，指的是工作经历里的那一行。2026 年的回顾如果只留较大的那一个词，就会改写材料。团队人数没有写，我不补一个编制。

产品事实可以短短带过，它们不负责把职称再升一档。上线 4 类场景，运营场景覆盖 80% 以上，覆盖口径没有再定义。对比过更自主的方案之后，采用的是 Workflow、单 Agent 和技能包。这是项目里记下的决策，不是另一套职位名称。

回顾的标题用两行，不用一行

所以这篇 2025 回顾的角色，写成两行。时间停在 3 月到 12 月，整段已经结束之后才在 2026 年 11 月 19 日写下来，不把 12 月的结果说成年度中途已经知道。案例在 AllyNext。

结果不给任何一行投票

两行角色还决定结果该怎么引用。写负责人时，我指的是项目条目里的那一行，包括路线被记在这个项目上。写深度参与时，我指的是工作经历没有把同一年写成更满的职权。4 类场景和 80% 以上的覆盖可以跟在任何一句后面，它们不投票给哪一行。覆盖口径没有再定义，所以 80% 以上也不帮任何一行补出一个团队人数。3 月到 12 月大约十个月。十个月结束之后再回顾，两行仍然并排。

材料没有分月上线表。因此这篇不说 7 月之前哪几类场景已经上线、12 月又多了哪几类。整段结束之后，只能说这一年留下 4 类和两行角色。中途的月份没有单独的结果可写。

十个月里可以引用的事实还有边界。覆盖 80% 以上没有定义场景怎么数，所以它不能变成团队规模，也不能变成我的个人工时。Workflow、单 Agent 和技能包是路线的名字，写在项目记录里。它支持「这条路线被记在项目上」，不支持把工作经历里的深度参与改成另一个动词。两行角色是这篇回顾的骨架。4 类场景可以挂在骨架上，骨架本身仍是那两行。项目条目和工作经历的差别，不靠别的年份来抹平。2025 年的回顾只处理这两行。深度参与不改成主导，负责人也不删掉。两行都保留，才算把 2025 年的角色写完。回顾的标题因此用两行，不用一行，并排留下即可。起止只有 3 月和 12 月，中间没有第三行角色。发表日是 2026 年 11 月 19 日，整段已经结束，中途月份仍然没有单独结果。

AllyNext ran from March to December 2025. Looking back, the role is two lines, and I keep both. The project entry says AI product owner. The work history writes the same stretch as deep participation across the AIGC agent chain. The owner line does not erase “deep participation.” The participation line does not cancel the owner line on the project entry.

Each line governs one way of speaking

“Owner” means the line in the project record. “Deep participation” means the line in the work history. A 2026 retrospective that kept only the larger word would rewrite the record. Headcount is not written, and I do not add a staffing figure.

The product facts can stay short. They do not promote the title. Four scene types shipped, with operations-scene coverage of 80%+ and no further definition of coverage. After comparing more autonomous options, the design was a workflow, a single agent, and a skill pack. That is a decision recorded on the project, not another job title.

The retrospective uses both lines

So the role in this 2025 retrospective is two lines. The dates stop at March through December. The piece is written on 19 November 2026, after that stretch had ended. December’s results are not described as already known in the middle of the year. The case is AllyNext.

Results do not vote for either line

The two lines also decide how a result is cited. “Owner” points at the project-entry line, including the route recorded on that project. “Deep participation” points at the work history, which does not describe the same year with a fuller authority. Four scenes and coverage of 80%+ can follow either sentence. They do not vote for one line. Coverage is not further defined, so 80%+ does not fill in a team size for either line. March to December is about ten months. After those ten months, the retrospective still keeps the two lines side by side.

The record has no monthly launch table. This piece therefore does not say which scenes were live before July and which arrived by December. After the stretch ended, what can be said is four scene types and two role lines. The months in between have no separate result to write.

Facts that can be cited across the ten months still have a boundary. Coverage of 80%+ does not define how a scene is counted, so it cannot become a team size or my personal hours. Workflow, single agent, and skill pack are the name of the route, written in the project record. That supports “this route is recorded on the project.” It does not support changing “deep participation” in the work history into another verb. The two role lines are the frame of this retrospective. Four scene types can hang on that frame. The frame is still the two lines. The difference between the project entry and the work history is not smoothed over by another year. This 2025 retrospective deals only with those two lines. The publication date is 19 November 2026, the stretch has ended, and the months in the middle still have no separate result.
