点击率旁边写着「配合」 The click rate is marked as work alongside an experiment

AllyNext 在 2025 年 3 月到 12 月有一条点击率记录：配合大规模 A/B，产出素材的平均 CTR 是 3%，同比提升 150%。实验前的 CTR 绝对值没有给出。回看时，我先读「配合」这两个字。

配合限定了实验是谁的

配合，说的是测试不是我单独设计的。3% 是那场实验里产出素材的平均点击率。150% 是同比，不是相对一个写出来的实验前基数。两句不能收成「我设计了一场把点击率做到 3% 的实验」。没有实验前的绝对值，同比就停在同比。

同一年里还有别的数字，它们不是这一次点击率。视频制作约 2 天到 20 分钟，Listing 生成约 3 小时到 15 分钟，量的是履约耗时。简历把这两则概括为交付时间最高下降 99%，那是概括，不是第三次点击实验。一次采纳率是另一个区间。成本下降是另一口径。点击率只说明配合实验时素材的点击表现。

整段结束之后才写

我不把 3% 和 150% 写成年中某一月已经落定的结果，因为材料没有分月。项目条目是 AI 产品负责人，工作经历是深度参与，两行都留着。案例在 AllyNext。

150% 换不成一个起点

同比 150% 要有上年的基数，才能换成绝对变化。基数没写，150% 就不能说成「从百分之几到 3%」。3% 本身是平均 CTR，不是峰值。配合大规模实验，说明这些素材来自那次测试的产出，不是全站所有素材的自然点击。3% 的范围因此是实验中的产出素材。把范围说大，就超出材料。

履约耗时和采纳率留在各自的句子里，只为标明它们不是点击率的分母，也不是实验前的 CTR。12 月才结束的项目没有月度点击序列。回顾就停在整段的这一行，不挑一个年中的月份把 3% 安上去。

读这条记录的顺序也固定。先看动词是不是配合，再看 3% 是平均还是峰值，然后看 150% 有没有实验前的绝对值。三步都成立，才能说这是我单独设计、并把点击率从某个数做到 3% 的实验。现在第一步就不成立，后面又缺少峰值和基数。所以 2025 年的这一行，停在配合、平均 3%、同比 150%、实验前绝对值未写。年中任何一个月都没有单独的点击率可填。三个词必须同时在场：拿掉配合，3% 会被读成我自己的实验；拿掉同比，150% 会被读成相对实验前基数。平均 CTR 的主语是实验产出的素材，同比的主语是上一年，配合的主语是我和那场测试的关系。三个主语都留着，不并成一句。不把 3% 写成峰值，也不把 150% 写成实验前到实验后的差值。材料里没有那种差值，所以不写。整段从 3 月记到 12 月，仍然不是某一个年中月份的快照。

AllyNext, from March to December 2025, has one click-rate line: alongside large-scale A/B tests, average CTR of the produced assets was 3%, up 150% year over year. The pre-test CTR is not given. Looking back, I read the word “alongside” first.

Alongside limits whose experiment it was

Alongside means I did not design the test alone. 3% is the average click rate of assets in that experiment. 150% is year over year, not a change against a written pre-test base. The two sentences do not become “I designed an experiment that took click rate to 3%.” Without a pre-test absolute, the year-over-year figure stays year over year.

The same year has other numbers, and they are not this click rate. Video production from about 2 days to 20 minutes, and Listing generation from about 3 hours to 15 minutes, measure turnaround. The resume summarizes those two as delivery time down by as much as 99%. That is a summary, not a third click experiment. First-pass acceptance is another range. The cost drop is another measure. Click rate only describes how assets performed alongside the experiment.

Written after the stretch had ended

I do not write 3% and 150% as results already fixed in some month mid-year, because the record has no monthly split. The project entry says AI product owner, the work history says deep participation, and both lines stay. The case is AllyNext.

150% does not become a starting point

A 150% year-over-year rise needs last year’s base before it becomes an absolute change. The base is unwritten, so 150% cannot be said as “from some percent to 3%.” 3% itself is an average CTR, not a peak. Alongside a large experiment means these assets are the experiment’s output, not the natural clicks of every asset on the site. The scope of 3% is therefore assets produced in the experiment. Widening that scope goes past the record.

Turnaround and acceptance stay in their own sentences only to mark that they are not the denominator of click rate and not the pre-test CTR. A project that ended in December has no monthly click series. The retrospective stops on that one line for the whole stretch. It does not pick a mid-year month and place 3% there.

The reading order is fixed. First whether the verb is “alongside,” then whether 3% is an average or a peak, then whether 150% has a pre-test absolute. Only if all three hold can the line become an experiment I designed alone that took click rate from a known number to 3%. The first step already fails, and the later steps lack a peak and a base. So the 2025 line stops at alongside, average 3%, 150% year over year, and no pre-test absolute. No month in the middle of the year has a separate click rate to fill in. Three words have to stay together. Remove “alongside,” and 3% gets read as my own experiment. Remove the year-over-year identity, and 150% gets read against a pre-test base. The line runs from March through December. It is still not a snapshot of one month in the middle of the year.
