先有 Owner 和有效期，再让模型去检索 Assign an owner and an expiry before the model retrieves

回答站不站得住，先看系统被允许知道什么，这些知识谁负责，过期了谁来换，以及改完 Prompt 之后用什么回归。换一代模型，解决不了无主、过期和不可追溯。

四域要有主，也要有期限

2024 年 2 月到 12 月的多语言电商客服，是我承接上级立项之后担任执行负责人的项目，不是我独立立项。一线要面对 1000 多种产品、上千页说明书和合规要求，新人上手要 5 到 6 个月。我执行推动的是公司大模型底座上、知识图谱和 RAG 双模式的客服助理。

知识库按产品、话术、流程、合规四个域来建，条目 10000 以上，并建立「知识 Owner + 有效期」。切片和向量化与算法一起定，Top-3 命中率从 68% 到 86%。Prompt 有模板库和版本管理。评测是 6 维人工评测，加上 800 条离线样本；6 个维度的名称没有单列。Prompt 变更前做全量回归。回答准确率从 62% 到 89%，口径是人工标注，评测体系迭代到 V4。版本主题是 V1 MVP、V2 功能稳定、V3 业务反馈加数据漏斗双轨、V4 模型微调，各版没有月份，也没有分版准确率。日均节省人工 200 次，30 天主动留存 90% 以上。检索资料时间降低 95%，提效 80% 以上，两则并陈，口径关系没有再定义。案例在 多语言客服 RAG。

库小、要精确命中时，检索可以更轻

AllyNext 的平台知识库规模小、结构规整，又要精确命中，所以用关键词融合检索加分段读取，做成轻量 RAG，暂时不上向量库。这是当时约束下的选择，用来服务按 SOP 生成可投产内容，不拿来概括所有检索方案。项目条目是 AI 产品负责人，工作经历是深度参与，时间是 2025 年 3 月到 12 月。见 AllyNext。

尺子要能复盘，结论要能回到条款

经营分析 Copilot 里，我把《运营经营指标体系白皮书 V1.0》当成同一把尺子：统一 5 个系统里 200 多个指标口径，既给可信回答用，也给效果评测用，并推动 8 张 ADS 表和血缘。那是 2023 年 7 月到 12 月、我作为 AI 产品经理执行落地的工作，写在 Copilot。

商舟把利润口径、合规规则和历史结论放进知识库，按步骤检索。结论回溯到数据和规则条款，关键结论的数据可追溯率 99%。规则随平台政策和 Badcase 更新，所以政策变了，知识有地方改，而不是整库重新灌一次。分析报告和 Listing 有分层评测。我在这里关心的是检索之前的条件：知识有主、有期限，回答和评测用同一把尺子。角色是 AI 产品负责人。见 商舟。

命中率、准确率、留存，不要读成一个数

Top-3 从 68% 到 86%，量的是检索把相关条目带进前三的比例。回答准确率从 62% 到 89%，量的是人工标注下、评测走到 V4 时的回答。日均节省 200 次人工，和 30 天主动留存 90% 以上，量的是使用。检索时间降低 95% 和提效 80% 以上并陈，因为两者的计算关系没有再写。我把它们分开，避免用命中率代替准确率，或用留存代替治理是否还在生效。四域合计 10000 条以上，Owner 和有效期要回答的是规则变更之后，条目还有没有人负责、有没有期限。回归要回答的是这次 Prompt 改动有没有把已经量过的质量打回去。

Whether an answer holds depends first on what the system is allowed to know, who owns that knowledge, who replaces it when it expires, and what regression runs after a prompt change. A newer model does not fix knowledge with no owner, no expiry, or no trace.

Four domains need an owner and a term

The multilingual ecommerce support assistant, from February to December 2024, was a project chartered above me. I was the execution owner, not the person who opened the charter. Front-line staff faced 1,000+ products and thousands of pages of manuals and compliance, and a new hire needed 5 to 6 months to get up to speed. I pushed an assistant on the company’s model base, driven by both a knowledge graph and RAG.

The knowledge base is four domains: product, scripts, process, and compliance, with 10,000+ entries, and an owner plus an expiry on the knowledge. Chunking and embedding were set with the algorithm team. Top-3 hit rate went from 68% to 86%. Prompts have a template library and version control. Evaluation is a 6-dimension human review plus 800 offline samples. The six dimension names are not listed separately. A full regression runs before a prompt change. Answer accuracy went from 62% to 89%, human-labeled, with the evaluation system through V4. The version themes are V1 MVP, V2 functional stability, V3 business feedback plus a data-funnel track, and V4 model tuning. There are no months per version and no accuracy per version. About 200 manual lookups a day were saved, and 30-day active retention is 90%+. Time spent searching materials fell 95%, and efficiency rose 80%+. Both figures stand side by side; how they relate is not further defined. The case is the support RAG.

When the library is small and the hit must be exact, retrieval can stay light

AllyNext’s platform knowledge base was small, regularly structured, and needed exact hits, so retrieval was keyword fusion plus segmented reads: a light RAG, and no vector store for the time being. That was a choice under those constraints, in service of SOP-based content fit to publish. It is not a general verdict on retrieval. The project entry says AI product owner; the work history says deep participation; the dates are March to December 2025. See AllyNext.

The ruler has to support a replay, and a conclusion has to reach a clause

On the operations Copilot I treated the metrics white paper, V1.0, as one ruler: it unifies 200+ metric definitions across 5 systems for both trustworthy answers and evaluation, and I pushed 8 ADS tables with lineage. That was execution work as an AI product manager, July to December 2023, written in the Copilot.

Shangzhou puts profit definitions, compliance rules, and past conclusions into the knowledge base and retrieves them by step. A conclusion traces back to data and to a rule clause. Traceability of key conclusions is 99%. Rules update with platform policy and with bad cases, so a policy change has a place to land instead of a full reload of the library. Reports and Listings have layered evaluation. What I care about here is the condition before retrieval: knowledge has an owner and a term, and answers and evaluation share one ruler. My role was AI product owner. See Shangzhou.

Hit rate, accuracy, and retention are not one number

Top-3 moving from 68% to 86% measures how often retrieval brings a relevant entry into the first three. Answer accuracy from 62% to 89% measures human-labeled answers once evaluation has reached V4. About 200 manual lookups saved per day, and 30-day active retention of 90%+, measure use. A 95% drop in search time and an efficiency gain of 80%+ stand side by side because their arithmetic relation is not further written. I keep them apart, so hit rate is not asked to stand in for accuracy, and retention is not asked to stand in for whether governance is still in force. The four domains together are 10,000+ entries. Owner and expiry answer whether, after a rule change, an entry still has someone responsible and still has a term. Regression answers whether this prompt change pushed back a quality that had already been measured.
