Inbox 工作流：低摩擦把点子收进系统 Inbox workflow: capture ideas before they evaporate

做 Agent 和写实践文时，想法往往在地铁、会议缝隙里冒出来。如果入口太重——打开笔记软件、选文件夹、起标题——它就死在半路。我给自己定的原则是：先收，后分。漂亮的分类体系，优先级永远排在「先留下钩子」之后。

低摩擦长什么样

一个永远打开的 Inbox：手机快捷指令、桌面剪贴板、或聊天里丢给自己的一句话。格式随便，标签以后再说。关键是三秒内能留下「钩子」——一个动词 + 一个对象就够，例如「评测台加约束用例」「作战室：诊断官禁止鸡汤」。写不全没关系，钩子在，回头就能补。

我刻意不在捕获时做归档决策。捕获阶段唯一的成功标准是：稍后的自己还能看懂这是什么。

和 Agent 工作怎么接

Inbox 不是第二大脑，只是进料口。每周我会扫一遍：能变成用例的进评测台；能变成角色契约的进作战室备忘；能写成文的进博客草稿；需要长期保留的链进笔记地图。剩下的归档或删掉，绝不假装「以后有空整理」。

我学到什么

收集系统的对手不是「懒」，而是决策疲劳。把分类决策推迟到有整块时间时，捕获率会上去；捕获率上去，实践文和 Demo 才有原材料，而不是靠灵感表演。Agent 项目尤其吃这个：好用例几乎都来自某次现场踩坑留下的半句话。

Ideas for agents and practice essays show up in gaps—commutes, the minute after a meeting. If capture is heavy (open the app, pick a folder, invent a title), the idea dies mid-air. My rule: capture first, sort later. Pretty taxonomy always ranks after “leave a hook.”

What low friction looks like

One always-on inbox: a phone shortcut, a desktop clip, or a line I message myself. Formatting is optional; tags wait. The bar is three seconds to leave a hook—a verb plus an object, e.g. “eval lab: add constraint cases” or “war room: diagnosis role bans pep talk.” Incomplete is fine if Future Me can parse the intent.

I refuse filing decisions at capture time. The only success criterion then is: later-me still understands what this was.

Wiring it to agent work

Inbox is not a second brain; it is a feed. Weekly I triage: cases go to the eval lab, role contracts to war-room notes, essay seeds to blog drafts, keepers into the notes map. The rest archive or delete—no theater of “I’ll organize later.”

What I learned

The enemy of capture is not laziness; it is decision fatigue. Deferring taxonomy until you have a real block of time raises capture rate—and that rate feeds demos and essays, not performative inspiration. Agent work especially: the best cases often start as half a sentence from a live failure.
