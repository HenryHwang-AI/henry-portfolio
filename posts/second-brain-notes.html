第二大脑式笔记：少建库，多写链接 Second-brain notes: fewer databases, more links

我试过把一切丢进华丽的数据库字段，结果维护字段比写内容还累。现在站内的笔记入口故意很轻：地图式导航，连向实践主题，而不是再造一个 CMS。库可以以后再长，链接必须现在就有。

我怎么组织

节点按「问题」而不是按「工具名」：评测、引用、多 Agent 协作、集成原则、捕获流。一篇实践文、一个 Demo、一条 Inbox 钩子，尽量互相指认。链接失败了也没关系——断链比空库诚实，至少告诉你这里曾经想接通。

我也不追求节点数量。宁可二十个高频回访的问题，也不要两千个只收藏不回流的网页剪藏。

和博客的关系

笔记是毛坯，博客是对外抛光后的切片。同一主题可以先在笔记里堆矛盾，等结论稳了再写成双语文章挂到博客分类里。评测台、引溯、作战室这几篇，都是先在笔记里吵清楚，再搬到博客的「技术 / 实践 / 随笔」。

我学到什么

第二大脑的密度来自回流：从 Demo 打回笔记，从笔记长出文章，从文章反改 Agent 契约。建库很爽，写链接很烦——但我选烦的那个，因为它真的复合利息。若你也在搭个人站，先把笔记页做成入口，再慢慢填自己的 URL，比一上来设计完美本体更接近能用。链接写得越多，Inbox 里那些半句话才知道该流向哪一篇实践。

站内入口：笔记 · 相关文：评测台 · 引溯 · 作战室

I tried stuffing everything into fancy database properties and ended up maintaining schema instead of writing. The site’s notes entry stays light on purpose: a map of practice themes, not another CMS. Databases can grow later; links have to exist now.

How I organize

Nodes are named by problems, not tool brands: eval, citation, multi-agent collaboration, integration rules, capture flow. A practice essay, a demo, and an inbox hook should point at each other. Broken links beat empty databases—they at least admit a connection was intended.

I do not chase node count. Twenty problems I revisit beat two thousand clippings that never loop back.

Relation to the blog

Notes are raw stock; blog posts are polished slices for others. I stockpile contradictions in notes, then cut a bilingual essay once the claim stabilizes. The eval, citation, and war-room pieces all argued with themselves in notes before landing under Technical / Practice / Reflection.

What I learned

Second-brain density comes from loops: demos feed notes, notes grow essays, essays rewrite agent contracts. Building databases feels great; writing links feels annoying—I pick annoying, because that is where the interest compounds. If you are building a personal site, ship a notes door first and fill URLs slowly; that beats designing a perfect ontology up front.
