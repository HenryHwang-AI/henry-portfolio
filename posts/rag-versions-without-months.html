四版只有主题，我排不出月份 Four versions have themes, and no months

2024 年 2 月到 12 月，多语言电商客服大约十一个月。我是执行负责人。项目由上级立项，不是我独立立项。一线要面对 1000 多种产品、上千页说明书和合规要求，新人上手要 5 到 6 个月。这是问题的规模。节奏要另说。

四句主题，没有日历

材料里有四版，只有主题。V1 是 MVP。V2 是功能稳定。V3 是业务反馈，加上数据漏斗的双轨。V4 是模型微调。没有各版的月份，也没有各版的准确率。回答准确率从 62% 到 89%，口径是人工标注，评测体系迭代到 V4。终点写在 V4 上，不写在某个月上。

Top-3 命中率从 68% 到 86%，来自与算法一起定的切片和向量化，同样没有按月拆开。日均节省人工 200 次，30 天主动留存 90% 以上，是使用读数，不是版本表上的格子。

十一个月只够写出顺序

回看 2024，我能排列的是这四句主题的先后。十一个月足够让这些主题发生过，不足以补出「几月到几月」。谁要月份，材料里没有，这篇也不编。准确率的 89% 只和「评测到 V4」绑在一起。

角色保持执行负责人。上级立项这一句留在回顾的开头，避免把十一个月写成自己开的项目。案例在 多语言客服 RAG。

每个主题只支持一句

V1 只说明有过一版能开始用的起点。V2 只说明后来有过把功能稳住的一段。V3 只说明业务反馈和数据漏斗曾经被一起看。V4 只说明评测走到微调。四个词推不出每段几个月，也推不出 V2 或 V3 的准确率。62% 到 89% 是整段评测的两端，中间值没有。知识库 10000 条以上说明规模，不说明哪一版加进了多少条。十一个月只证明这些主题有时间发生，不证明它们均匀排开。

回看时，我把「没有月份」写成 2024 记录的一部分，而不是一个要在后来填上的空。谁需要日历，得另有材料。这篇没有。

执行负责人在这十一个月里能对节奏负责的，是主题被记下来，不是日历被补全。上级立项说明起点不是我定的。四版主题说明推进被分成四段来记。准确率的两端说明评测有起点和终点。三者放在一起，仍然产生不出月份。若问 V3 在夏天还是秋天，答案只能是材料没写。2024 的回顾把这个「没写」留在明处，不换成一个看起来整齐的时间表。2 月到 12 月是起止，中间怎么切没有第二层日期。执行负责人不因为十一个月的长度就获得一张分月表。命中率 68% 到 86%，准确率 62% 到 89%，日均 200 次和 30 天留存 90% 以上，也都没有月份，不能用来反推版本时间。V1 到 V4 的顺序可以记，间距不能记。评测到 V4 只说明终点落在第四个主题。看见 2 月到 12 月的起止，仍然补不出中间的月份。

From February to December 2024, the multilingual ecommerce support assistant ran about eleven months. I was the execution owner. The project was chartered above me. I did not open it. Front-line staff faced 1,000+ products and thousands of pages of manuals and compliance, and a new hire needed 5 to 6 months. That is the scale of the problem. Pace is a separate sentence.

Four themes, no calendar

The record has four versions, and only themes. V1 is an MVP. V2 is functional stability. V3 is business feedback plus a data-funnel track. V4 is model tuning. There are no months per version and no accuracy per version. Answer accuracy went from 62% to 89%, human-labeled, with the evaluation system through V4. The end point is tied to V4, not to a month.

Top-3 hit rate went from 68% to 86%, from chunking and embedding set with the algorithm team, and it is not split by month either. About 200 manual lookups saved per day, and 30-day active retention of 90%+, are usage readings. They are not cells on a version table.

Eleven months support an order, not a calendar

Looking back at 2024, what I can order is those four themes. Eleven months is enough for the themes to have happened. It is not enough to fill in “from which month to which month.” Where the record has no months, this retrospective does not add them. The 89% accuracy stays tied to “evaluation through V4.”

The role stays execution owner. The charter from above stays in the opening, so eleven months is not rewritten as a project I opened. The case is the support RAG.

Each theme supports one sentence

V1 only says there was a starting point that could be used. V2 only says there was a later stretch of making the functions hold. V3 only says business feedback and a data funnel were looked at together. V4 only says evaluation reached tuning. The four words do not yield how many months each took, and they do not yield accuracy at V2 or V3. From 62% to 89% is the two ends of the whole evaluation. The values in between are absent. A knowledge base of 10,000+ entries says scale. It does not say how many entries each version added. Eleven months only shows there was time for the themes to happen. It does not show they were spaced evenly.

Looking back, I write “no months” as part of the 2024 record, not as a blank to fill later. A calendar would need other material. This piece does not have it.

What an execution owner can answer for pace across those eleven months is that the themes were written down, not that a calendar was completed. The charter from above says I did not set the start. The four themes say the work was recorded in four stretches. The two ends of accuracy say evaluation had a start and an end. Together they still do not produce months. If someone asks whether V3 was summer or autumn, the answer is that the record does not say. This 2024 retrospective leaves that absence visible. It does not replace it with a tidy timetable. February to December is the start and end. How the middle is cut has no second layer of dates. An execution owner does not receive a monthly table just because the stretch lasted eleven months. The February-to-December span is visible, and the months in between are still not recoverable.
